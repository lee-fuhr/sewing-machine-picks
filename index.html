How we choose our picks

Our recommendations come from a mix of long-term owner feedback, repair-shop insights, and published reviews from professional sewists and home hobbyists. We don’t chase specs or claim to have tested every model ourselves; instead, we look at patterns in what real owners say after months and years of use, and we take seriously the machines that keep showing up in repair shop “frequently broken” lists. We focus on what actually goes wrong: tension issues, jammed bobbins, flimsy plastic gears, and customer service nightmares. A machine can look impressive on paper, but if owners consistently report the same failure a year in, it doesn’t make our list. We also weigh ease of threading, how easy it is to switch presser feet, and how willing brand repair networks are to help when things break.
